- [Streamlining Academic Management](https://sam.em.ufl.edu/) - Data, Reporting, Analytics Workgroup (2024-10-21 - Present)
    ``` 
    Streamlining Academic Management (SAM) is a university-wide initiative designed to make academic management seamless and empower student success while improving the efficiency of administrative tasks for faculty and staff.  

    SAM helps UF students progress efficiently through their degree programs and graduate on time by making it easier to find and register for the classes they need.  

    For faculty and staff, SAM reduces the time spent on complex academic planning and coordination, freeing them to focus on advising and supporting students rather than managing outdated, disconnected systems.  

    At the heart of SAM is the implementation of two modern, best-in-class platforms—Coursedog and Stellic. These tools replace aging systems that operate in silos, automating and integrating critical functions to deliver a more unified, future-ready experience.  
    ```

    ``` 
    The University of Florida's COMPASS (Campus-wide Modernization Program to Advance Student Services) implementation unified student services systems to benefit students as well as faculty and staff who use and need access to student-related information.  
    
    As a preeminent university at the forefront of online teaching and learning, UF needed student services systems that are agile and modern. COMPASS enabled UF to modernize, standardize and vastly improve the processes and information systems used to manage and deliver services to students, and the project's elements will offer exciting innovations and abilities in the areas of constituent relationship management (CRM), learning ecosystem, student data governance, master data management, reporting and analytics, ONE.UF and myUFL.
    ```
